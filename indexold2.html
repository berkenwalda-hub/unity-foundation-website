About Unity Foundation

At Unity Foundation, our story is one of visionary leadership and a commitment to radical inclusion. Founded by Ebrahim Moshiri, Unity Foundation was established with a grand vision: a world where everyone has equitable access to information and the tools to shape their own future.

We are not just an organization; we are a movement dedicated to building CommUNITY. Our mission is to champion open technology and forge paths to equal opportunities, transforming education, political systems, and society at large.

The Rosie Project™

A critical component of Unity Foundation's mission, The Rosie Project serves as our primary framework for talent and technology development. It embodies our "People First" posture—a permanent commitment to recruiting and developing emerging talent to solve the most pressing capability gaps facing our nation.

Within the Unity Foundation ecosystem, The Rosie Project acts as the engine that:

✓ Mobilizes Talent: Connects young professionals and students with mentors and mission-critical work.

✓ Bypasses Bureaucracy: Uses commercial best practices to build solutions outside the constraints of legacy systems.

✓ Proves Urgency: Acts as the proof of concept for how structured pipelines can solve hard problems in real-time.

Discovery Through Execution

From Problem Identification to Cross-Industry Transformation

The Rosie Project identified the burning platform. Unity Foundation built the institution to address it. Open Technology provides the infrastructure to scale it—not just across defense, but across every sector that shapes the quality of life, security, and opportunity of the nation.

"The Rosie Project is not a program. It is a posture—a permanent commitment to building the human and technical infrastructure that national readiness demands, one cohort at a time."

The Three Foundational Pillars

👥 People First

Recruit, develop, and connect emerging talent—particularly young professionals, students, and career-changers—with the mentors, organizations, and missions that need them most. We leverage structured on-ramps into defense, logistics, technology, and cross-industry careers.

💻 Technology as a Tool, Not a Barrier

Use commercial best practices and open development environments to build solutions outside the constraints of legacy procurement systems—then bring those solutions in. We solve first and comply second, reversing the traditional model that too often kills innovation before it starts.

🏗️ Process That Outlasts Personalities

Build systems, not programs. The Rosie Project was explicitly designed to be replicable across chapters and scalable across industries—a proof of concept that any organization, in any sector, can adopt to close its own talent and technology gaps.

The Universal Problem We Solve

The challenges that inspired The Rosie Project are not unique to the Department of Defense. Across every sector—healthcare, energy, finance, education, manufacturing, and public service—the same three crises converge:

⚡

Accelerating Complexity

Problems are outpacing the systems designed to solve them. Technology evolves faster than institutions can adapt, creating capability gaps that threaten competitiveness and security.

🏛️

Institutional Inertia

Bureaucratic structures slow the adoption of solutions that already exist. Legacy systems and procurement processes create barriers to innovation and rapid deployment.

🎓

The Generational Talent Gap

Emerging talent lacks structured pathways into mission-critical work. The next generation wants to serve but can't find meaningful on-ramps into impactful careers.

"The Rosie Project named these problems in a defense context. Unity Foundation solves them universally."

Cross-Industry Applications

🏥

Healthcare & Public Health

Problem: Critical shortages in clinical informatics, health IT, and care coordination talent.

Solution: Structured intern pipelines feeding hospital systems, public health agencies, and biotech firms with open-source EHR interoperability tools.

⚡

Energy & Climate Infrastructure

Problem: The green energy transition demands massive reskilling and new engineering talent pipelines.

Solution: Cohort-based programs embedded in utilities and clean energy startups, building open-source grid modeling tools.

🏦

Financial Services & FinTech

Problem: Regulatory complexity, cybersecurity threats, and AI adoption require talent that bridges compliance and technology.

Solution: Structured progression tracks with open banking APIs and shared compliance frameworks.

🎓

Education & Workforce Development

Problem: Higher education is disconnected from employer needs; students graduate without applied experience.

Solution: Growth Rings model becomes a national apprenticeship architecture with open courseware and credentialing systems.

🏗️

Manufacturing & Supply Chain

Problem: Supply chain fragility, logistics complexity, and the need for resilient systems exist far beyond DoD.

Solution: Coding competition model applied to commercial challenges with open-source visibility platforms.

🛡️

Defense & National Security

Problem: Near-peer threats and contested logistics demand faster IT development cycles.

Solution: The original vision—talent pipelines feeding USTRANSCOM, DLA, and combatant commands with open-source defense tools.

Open Technology: The Force Multiplier

Open Technology is not just a development philosophy—it is the structural backbone that makes the Unity Foundation model scalable, equitable, and durable across all sectors.

🧱

Shared Infrastructure

Don't build what already exists. Improve what everyone can use. Open-source platforms, APIs, and data standards mean every contribution compounds in value across the entire ecosystem.

🌍

Democratized Access

Open Technology removes financial and institutional barriers. A community college cohort can contribute to the same platform as a research university—and both contributions matter.

🔄

The Contribution Loop

Students enter, learn on open tools, contribute improvements back to the commons, build public portfolios, advance to the next tier, and mentor the next cohort. The commons grows stronger with every cycle.

🛡️

Resilience Through Transparency

No single point of failure, no vendor lock-in, and no capability that disappears when a contract ends. Built for longevity, not personality.

🤝

Cross-Sector Collaboration

Open standards and APIs allow the ecosystem to connect across sectors. A healthcare tool can be adapted by logistics; a defense algorithm can be repurposed for hospital staffing.

"This is not a defense program with civilian applications. This is not a workforce program with a technology component. This is a national capability-building architecture—and it belongs to every industry willing to invest in the next generation."
