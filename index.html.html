About Unity Foundation

At Unity Foundation, our story is one of visionary leadership and a commitment to radical inclusion. Founded by Ebrahim Moshiri, Unity Foundation was established with a grand vision: a world where everyone has equitable access to information and the tools to shape their own future.

We are not just an organization; we are a movement dedicated to building CommUNITY. Our mission is to champion open technology and forge paths to equal opportunities, transforming education, political systems, and society at large.

🎯

The Unity Ecosystem

A Universal Framework for National Capability-Building

Unity Foundation provides a national capability-building architecture designed to bridge the gap between human potential and technical complexity. Our model is built on a structured hierarchy that ensures longevity and scalability across all sectors.

Layer Function
🌱 Roots The foundation of Open Technology, shared APIs, and transparent infrastructure. We believe open computing liberates institutions from financial constraints and vendor lock-in.
🌳 Trunk The Unity Foundation Core System consisting of structured talent pipelines and mentor ecosystems that outlast any single individual.
🌿 Branches Industry Applications including our current focus on PreK-12 STEAM education in St. Louis, with applications in Healthcare, Energy, Finance, and Defense.
☀️ Canopy The Global Impact layer where knowledge transfer and open-source commons benefit society at large.
🔓

Open Technology

We ensure innovation is transparent, interoperable, and owned by the community through open-source tools and shared APIs.

🏗️

Structured Pipelines

Building talent pathways and mentor ecosystems designed for longevity and scalability across all sectors.

🌐

Sector-Agnostic Model

While focused on education, our Unity model applies to Healthcare, Energy, Finance, Defense, and beyond.

The Rosie Project™

A critical component of Unity Foundation's mission, The Rosie Project serves as our primary framework for talent and technology development. It embodies our "People First" posture—a permanent commitment to recruiting and developing emerging talent to solve the most pressing capability gaps facing our nation.

Within the Unity Foundation ecosystem, The Rosie Project acts as the engine that:

✓ Mobilizes Talent: Connects young professionals and students with mentors and mission-critical work.

✓ Bypasses Bureaucracy: Uses commercial best practices to build solutions outside the constraints of legacy systems.

✓ Proves Urgency: Acts as the proof of concept for how structured pipelines can solve hard problems in real-time.

Our Impact: Education & Empowerment

Transforming St. Louis through PreK-12 STEAM Education

Currently, Unity Foundation is deeply invested in the St. Louis community, focusing on PreK-12 STEAM education. We believe that open technology is the key to making classrooms vibrant, equitable, and truly innovative.

💻

Open Computing Integration

Developing PreK-12 curricula that weave math, English, and social studies with robotics, gaming, and media using open-source technology.

📚

Teacher & Student Empowerment

Training educators and guiding student projects to build vital STEM and Social-Emotional Learning (SEL) skills.

🎓

Media Literacy

Teaching K-12 students critical thinking and responsible digital citizenship with a nuanced understanding of digital media.

🧠

AI Ethics & Mindfulness

Every curriculum is crafted to instill ethical leadership, AI ethics, and mindfulness in the next generation.

🌟

Open Educational Resources

Advocating for Free Open Educational Resources (OER) to liberate schools from financial constraints.

👥

CommUNITY Governance

Building leadership teams dedicated to developing youth camps, community projects, and capstone experiences for underserved areas.
