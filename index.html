About Unity Foundation

At Unity Foundation, our story is one of visionary leadership and a commitment to radical inclusion. Founded by Ebrahim Moshiri, Unity Foundation was established with a grand vision: a world where everyone has equitable access to information and the tools to shape their own future.

We are not just an organization; we are a movement dedicated to building CommUNITY. Our mission is to champion open technology and forge paths to equal opportunities, transforming education, political systems, and society at large.

The Rosie Project™

A critical component of Unity Foundation's mission, The Rosie Project serves as our primary framework for talent and technology development. It embodies our "People First" posture—a permanent commitment to recruiting and developing emerging talent to solve the most pressing capability gaps facing our nation.

Within the Unity Foundation ecosystem, The Rosie Project acts as the engine that:

✓ Mobilizes Talent: Connects young professionals and students with mentors and mission-critical work.

✓ Bypasses Bureaucracy: Uses commercial best practices to build solutions outside the constraints of legacy systems.

✓ Proves Urgency: Acts as the proof of concept for how structured pipelines can solve hard problems in real-time.

Discovery Through Execution

From Problem Identification to Cross-Industry Transformation

The Rosie Project is not a program. It is a posture—a permanent commitment to building the human and technical infrastructure that national readiness demands, one cohort at a time.
